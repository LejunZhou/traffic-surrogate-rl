## 2. E0 — characterisation (`_progress/m8_e0_characterisation.json`, ledger `m8_e0`)
404 rollouts (20 insertion + 12 × 11 constant + 12 × 21 schedules), 11 min wall-clock.

**(i) Insertion.** Where no merge breakdown occurs, served / offered = 0.982–0.988 with 0
pending mainline vehicles and 0 teleports on every profile; the residual ≈ 1.5 % is the
vehicles still on the road at t = 60 min. Where the peak total exceeds capacity at
pass-through (9 of 20 profiles, peak 2530–2860 vph) the merge breaks down and the
mainline entry backs up (60–529 pending vehicles at the end, served / offered
0.73–0.92): that is the physical backlog the metering policy is there to prevent, and
it is what the TTS reward counts (§3).

**(ii) Capacity map.** Breakdown first appears at u = 0.3 (480 vph release) on every
profile whose peak total exceeds ≈ 2500 vph and never on the others; profiles with
peak < 2500 are optimally served by the largest u (pass-through).

**(iii) Dynamic-vs-constant margin.** First pass (store-and-flush with flush at u = 1):
every schedule lost to the best constant, because flushing 1600 vph on top of a
1400–1500 vph mainline after the peak jams the merge. Second pass added
capacity-tracking feedforward schedules and moderate flush rates. Under the M7
three-term reward the margins stayed within ±17 return units (1/10 peaked profiles
passed), although **TTS** drops by 19–65 veh h on the storage-mandatory profiles:

| profile | peak total | best constant (TTS) | best schedule (TTS) | TTS saved |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| e0[1] step/surge | 2786 | u 0.5 (143.3) | feedforward C 2300 (94.0) | 49.3 |
| e0[4] step/const | 2677 | u 0.6 (162.3) | feedforward C 2400 (97.2) | 65.1 |
| e0[5] peak/surge | 2886 | u 0.8 (95.9) | store 0.2 / flush 0.5 (68.0) | 27.9 |

| e0[8] peak/surge | 2635 | u 0.8 (76.8) | store 0.2 / flush 0.5 (57.7) | 19.1 |

Fitting the three-term weights to TTS over the 384 sweep rollouts (non-negative least
squares, `_progress/m8_e0_term_sums.json`) gives δ 0.805 / β 1 / γ 0.106 with R² 0.89,
but the per-profile ranking still puts constants first: the form has no term for the
delay of the mainline backlog (a jam's lost outflow is counted once per step, its
victims wait for the rest of the peak).

## 3. Decision: TTS-form training reward (deviation from draft D10)
`configs/rl/ppo_common.yaml` uses `reward.form: tts`:
r_k = −(N_k + Q_k + P_k)·Δt/3600 with N_k = Σ_i ρ_{i,k} Δx (vehicles on the mainline),
Q_k the ramp queue and P_k the mainline backlog by conservation
(offered − served − N − Q, ≥ 0). Episode return = −TTS in veh h. Every quantity exists in
both environments from (ρ, q_out, Q, d, r), so the reward is identical by construction
(`rl.reward.backlog_estimate`; SumoEnv logs the true pending count next to the estimate).
The three-term form stays as the "old reward" ablation (`configs/rl/reward_three_term.yaml`,
E7). Re-scored under the TTS reward (`scripts/run_scenario_characterisation.py --reward-config`,
no new SUMO), the E0 gate reads:

| profile | peak | best u (return) | best schedule (return) | margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| e0[1] | 2786 | 0.2 (−167.4) | ff C2300 (−109.3) | +58.1 |
| e0[4] | 2677 | 0.3 (−192.9) | ff C2400 (−120.0) | +72.9 |
| e0[5] | 2886 | 0.2 (−99.9) | store 0.2 / flush 0.5 (−76.5) | +23.4 |
| e0[7] | 2542 | 0.2 (−112.6) | store 0.2 / flush 0.5 (−85.9) | +26.6 |
| e0[8] | 2635 | 0.3 (−100.8) | store 0.2 / flush 0.5 (−64.0) | +36.8 |
| e0[0] | 2612 | 1.0 (−40.5) | (no jam at any u) | −12.7 |
| e0[2] | 2508 | 1.0 (−34.4) | (no jam) | −0.1 |

Gate on the storage-mandatory profiles (peak total > 2500): **5/7 passed** (the two
misses never jam at pass-through, so there is nothing to win). Over all peaked
profiles 5/10: the other five have peak totals below capacity and a pass-through
constant is optimal by design. The family is therefore kept as specified; roughly half
of the training family needs storage and half needs no metering, which is the
mix the policy should learn to tell apart.

## 4. Known limitations / open items
- The insertion residual (≈ 1.5 % of offered vehicles still on the road at t = T) and
  the 2–8 vehicles in ramp transit are not in N_k; both environments ignore them alike.
- E0 profiles come from the training family (`e0` seed set), not from V, so no
  validation profile is in the surrogate's training data.
- The draft's "mainline peak must end by minute 45" is implemented for the peak and
  step families; OOD profiles (plateau, double peak) do not follow it by design.
